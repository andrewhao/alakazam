# Initial Concept

AI-powered intelligent document renaming with adaptive type system.

# Product Definition: Alakazam

## Vision
Alakazam empowers Home Office Power Users by transforming chaotic document folders into perfectly searchable archives with zero manual effort. It uses advanced AI to understand document content and provide intelligent, consistent naming.

## Core Value Propositions
- **Perfect Searchability**: Consistent, descriptive filenames (ISO date + context) ensure documents are easily found.
- **Zero Manual Effort**: Automated AI-driven analysis replaces tedious manual renaming.
- **Adaptive Organization**: Learns and suggests new document types, becoming smarter with usage.

## Functional Goals
- **AI Analysis**: Supports multiple models (Anthropic, OpenAI) with tunable parameters for optimal accuracy.
- **High-Performance Batch Processing**: Optimized for large document sets, ensuring scalability.
- **Intelligent Tracking**: Prevents reprocessing of already renamed files.

## Target Audience
- **Home Office Power Users**: Individuals managing significant volumes of personal or professional digital paper trails who need a structured and searchable filing system.

## Roadmap & Future Directions
- **Built-in OCR Support**: Direct processing of images and scanned documents without pre-OCR steps.
- **Workflow Automation**: Listening for new files in specific locations (e.g., Dropbox folders) for real-time processing.
- **Enhanced Model Tuning**: Providing deeper control over AI parameters for specialized document sets.
